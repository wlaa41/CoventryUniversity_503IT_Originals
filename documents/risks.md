## Lessons Learned

The highest risks (scope, delays, bugs) were all mitigated through structured communication — daily WhatsApp updates and documented sprint goals ensured the team caught problems early rather than discovering them at submission. The Safari audio bug (Risk 8) was found during Sprint 5 testing rather than on submission day, demonstrating the value of planned testing cycles.
