
Concept Summary

A fast-paced educational mini-game where players answer questions and complete challenges to earn points and progress through levels.

Core Mechanics
Quiz/trivia challenges
Timed reactions
Score and combo system
Increasing difficulty per level
Game Flow

Start Menu → Tutorial → Gameplay → Score Screen → Next Level / Retry

Win/Lose Conditions
Win by reaching target score or completing all questions
Lose when time runs out or too many wrong answers are made
Reference Games
Fruit Ninja
Trivia Crack
Kahoot!
