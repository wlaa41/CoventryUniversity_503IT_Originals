🔐 Tip: Always use a unique password for every account you create.

🔐 Cyber Ethics & Digital Safety

🎣 Phishing Attacks

Criminals send fake emails pretending to be trusted organisations. Always verify the sender and never click suspicious links.

🔑 Strong Passwords

Use 12+ characters mixing uppercase, lowercase, numbers and symbols. Never reuse passwords. Use a password manager.

🛡️ Two-Factor Authentication

2FA adds a second verification step beyond your password. Even if your password is stolen, attackers can't access your account.

🦠 Malware & Ransomware

Malicious software that damages or encrypts your files. Keep all software updated and install reputable antivirus protection.

🕵️ Social Engineering

Attackers manipulate people psychologically into revealing confidential info. Always verify identities before sharing details.

🌐 Safe Browsing

Always check for HTTPS and the padlock icon. Use a VPN on public Wi-Fi to encrypt your internet connection.

💾 GDPR & Data Privacy

Under UK GDPR you have the right to request deletion of your personal data from any organisation at any time.

⚖️ Computer Misuse Act 1990

Unauthorised access to computer systems is a criminal offence in the UK, punishable with up to 10 years imprisonment.
